1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
During June 2025, overall project cumulative physical progress reached 34.2% against
a baseline planned progress of 41.0% (Schedule Variance: -6.8%). Production across
Spread A was constrained by welder shortages, radiography inspection cycle lags,
and localized root pass porosity repairs. Special crossings were severely impacted
by an unexpected borehole collapse and drilling mud loss at the CH 8+400 river HDD.
Spread B pre-welding activities (ROW grading and stringing) advanced satisfactorily.
Station civil works at PS-1 were halted pending non-conforming concrete core results.
