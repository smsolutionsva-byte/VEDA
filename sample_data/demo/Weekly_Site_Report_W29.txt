TRANS-RIDGE PIPELINE - SECTION 4
WEEKLY SITE PROGRESS REPORT
Week 29 - Period ending 18 July 2025
Prepared by: Site Construction Manager (R. Iyer)
Contractor: Ridgeline Constructors / Apex Pipeline Services

1. SUMMARY
Overall physical progress at cut-off reached approximately 41% against a
planned 50% (variance of -9.0%). Spread B mainline welding commenced. HDD pilot
hole drilling at the river crossing was restarted successfully with good mud
returns. Coating progress on Spread A was strong throughout the week.

2. PIPELINE - SPREAD A (CH 0+000 to 12+000)
Mainline welding reached ~51% (6,120 m completed) with 510 production joints
accepted. Radiography backlog has normalized to 3 working days behind the weld
front; NCR-2025-014 has been formally closed by QA (K. Bhatt).
Field joint coating achieved excellent progress, reaching 48% (5,760 m) with
dual induction preheating crews working between CH 4+500 and 5+800.
Trenching is at 84% (10,080 m). First pipeline lowering-in and padding
operations commenced between CH 0+500 and 1+800 (1.3 km accepted).

3. PIPELINE - SPREAD B (CH 12+000 to 24+000)
Spread B mainline welding commenced on 14 July with Crew CW-04 operating from
CH 12+000; 48 joints (580 m, ~5% of spread) completed with zero weld repairs.
Stringing advanced to 76% (9,120 m strung up to CH 21+120). Cold field bending
progressed to 38% (4,560 m). Trenching reached 29% (3,480 m excavated to
CH 15+480) with two excavator crews working in rock-free terrain.

4. SPECIAL CROSSINGS
HDD River Crossing (CH 8+400): Pilot hole re-drilling restarted on 15 July
utilizing an 8-3/4" mud motor and gyro steering tool with polymer-fortified
bentonite drilling fluid. Drill bit navigated successfully through the grouted
gravel palaeochannel without fluid loss. Cumulative pilot bore reached 320 m
of the 780 m crossing profile (41% of pilot bore) with 100% drilling fluid
returns to the recycling pit.

5. STATION WORKS
PS-1 (Pump Station 1): Structural collar reinforcement and formwork for slab
F-03 completed. Concrete pour executed on 16 July utilizing C35/45 high-early-
strength concrete mix. 3-day cylinder compressive tests broke at 28.2 MPa,
exceeding interim design threshold. Grouting of compressor skid anchor pocket
sleeves scheduled for early next week to allow mechanical staging.

6. TESTING AND COMMISSIONING
Hydrotest Section 1 test plan revision approved by client. Water fill routing
and filtration skid location at CH 0+000 confirmed. Pre-cleaning pigging
sequence drafted.

7. LABOUR AND PLANT
Total site manpower reached 256 personnel. Equipment fleet fully operational
with 20 excavators, 7 sidebooms, 2 bending machines, and 1 HDD spread.

8. WEATHER
Dry and sunny throughout the week; zero weather downtime recorded.

9. KEY CONCERNS RAISED BY SITE
- 24-hour drilling roster required for HDD reaming passes to prevent mud gelation.
- Procurement delivery dates for 24" sectionalizing ball valves for block valve
  stations require client expediting.
- Lowering-in and tie-in sequencing must be maintained to stay within trench
  stability time limits before seasonal late-summer rains.
