TRANS-RIDGE PIPELINE - SECTION 4
WEEKLY SITE PROGRESS REPORT
Week 27 - Period ending 04 July 2025
Prepared by: Site Construction Manager (R. Iyer)
Contractor: Ridgeline Constructors / Apex Pipeline Services

1. SUMMARY
Overall physical progress at cut-off stands at approximately 37% against a
planned 44% (variance of -7.0%). Pacing activities remain Spread A mainline
welding/radiography sequencing and the river crossing HDD works at CH 8+400.
Spread B trenching has commenced.

2. PIPELINE - SPREAD A (CH 0+000 to 12+000)
Stringing remains 100% complete. Bending advanced to 78% (cumulative 9,360 m).
Mainline welding reached ~42% (5,040 m) with crews CW-01 and CW-02 active.
Weld repairs on NCR-2025-011 (CH 5+400 root porosity) were completed,
re-examined by radiography, and formally accepted by QA (K. Bhatt).
Subcontractor Apex NDT mobilized the second radiography crew on 02 July;
darkroom calibration and local radiation safety clearance were completed, but
the crew has not yet achieved productive throughput. Radiography backlog is
currently 9 working days behind welding (down from 11 days).
Field joint coating (FJC) is starting to catch up, reaching 31% (3,720 m) with
crew CW-03 applying heat-shrink sleeves following closure of NCR-2025-012.
Trenching progress is accepted at 76% (9,120 m).

3. PIPELINE - SPREAD B (CH 12+000 to 24+000)
ROW grading reached 92%. Stringing advanced steadily to ~55% (6,600 m strung,
currently at CH 18+600) with pipe haulage running smoothly. Cold field bending
commenced on 30 June and stands at 15% (1,800 m bent). Trenching operations
started on 01 July in soft clay/sandstone sections between CH 12+000 and
12+850 (~7% of spread). Mainline welding crew CW-04 mobilization is on track
for mid-July.

4. SPECIAL CROSSINGS
HDD River Crossing (CH 8+400): Comprehensive geotechnical reassessment report
was received on 01 July from GeoEngineers Ltd. The report confirms an unmapped
buried cobble/gravel palaeochannel between CH 8+320 and CH 8+490 that triggered
the mud loss and borehole collapse (NCR-2025-015). HDD Lead A. Fernandes has
submitted a revised drilling programme adding approximately 3 weeks to the
crossing schedule. Contractor is mobilizing specialist high-density mud solids
recycling equipment and premium polymer additives, with delivery due 08 July.

5. STATION WORKS
PS-1 (Pump Station 1): Core test results (ASTM C42) for compressor foundation
slab F-03 returned compressive strengths averaging 21.5 MPa against the 28-day
design requirement of 30.0 MPa (NCR-2025-013). Civil consultant structural
review recommends a partial redesign involving an enlarged perimeter collar and
epoxy-grouted dowel grid rather than full demolition. Client approval is awaited.
Mechanical erection remains on hold.

6. TESTING AND COMMISSIONING
Pre-hydrotest engineering submittals for Test Section 1 (Spread A, CH 0+000 to
8+350) submitted for client review. Test manifold fabrication initiated at the
central pipe yard. River water abstraction permit application submitted.

7. LABOUR AND PLANT
Total site manpower averaged 228 personnel across all shifts. Two replacement
welders for crew CW-01 mobilized and completed ASME Sec IX testing. Equipment
on site: 18 excavators, 6 sidebooms, 2 cold bending machines, 1 HDD rig.

8. WEATHER
Four rain events during the period resulting in 1.5 shifts lost on field joint
coating and trench dewatering.

9. KEY CONCERNS RAISED BY SITE
- Revised HDD programme adds 3 weeks to the critical path, tightening the
  window ahead of the golden tie-in and November hydrotest deadline.
- Second RT crew must reach full daily production immediately to clear backlog.
- PS-1 foundation redesign approval urgently required to unblock station civil
  works and compressor skid installation.
- Timely pipe delivery to Spread B must be sustained to avoid stringing gaps.
