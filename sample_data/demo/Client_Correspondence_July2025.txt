ITEM 3: CLIENT NOTICE REGARDING HYDROTEST WATER ABSTRACTION WINDOW
Date: 10 July 2025 | Ref: TREC-RC-LTR-2025-0088
From: D. Campbell (Commissioning Manager, TransRidge Energy Corporation)
To: R. Iyer (Site Construction Manager, Ridgeline Constructors JV)

Dear Mr. Iyer,
Please be reminded that the environmental water abstraction permit issued by the
River Basin Authority limits pipeline hydrostatic test filling to the period
between 15 November and 05 December 2025 due to downstream agricultural flows.
Any failure to achieve mechanical completion of Spread A, Spread B, and the
CH 8+400 river crossing prior to 10 November 2025 will result in the loss of
this test window and a mandatory four-month deferral under environmental statute.
All tie-in welding and cleaning pig runs must be scheduled accordingly.
Regards,
David Campbell, Commissioning Manager
